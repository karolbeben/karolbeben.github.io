Karol Bęben

I build and test UAV systems, from avionics and sensors to software and flight experiments. My doctoral research explores cooperation between multiple UAVs through simulation and reinforcement learning.

I work at the Łukasiewicz Research Network – Institute of Aviation and pursue doctoral research at Warsaw University of Technology.

About

I integrate avionics, autopilots, electro-optical payloads and communications on fixed-wing and VTOL aircraft. My work also covers remote sensing, mission preparation, continuing airworthiness, and ground and flight tests, including BVLOS operations.

I am a qualified UAV pilot and instructor, with over ten years of UAV experience and more than 1,000 hours in UAV operations. Before working with UAVs, I worked as an aircraft mechanic.

Current research

My doctoral research examines how multiple UAVs can cooperate in low-altitude remote sensing. I study onboard computing, communication, simulation and learning-based control. Reinforcement learning is one of the methods I am investigating; testing approaches on real aircraft is an important goal. This work is in progress.
