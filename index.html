About

I have worked with unmanned aircraft for more than ten years. My engineering work includes configuring avionics, electronics, payloads and communications; preparing missions and required permissions; supporting continuing airworthiness; and conducting ground and flight tests. I have worked with DJI aircraft and custom UAVs using Pixhawk flight controllers.

Before specializing in UAVs, I worked as an aircraft mechanic. I am also a UAV instructor and a radio amateur (SP7UAV). These experiences shape my interest in how research methods perform on real aircraft.

Research interests

My doctoral work concerns control and mission optimization for groups of UAVs in low-altitude remote sensing, with an interest in reinforcement learning, mission coordination and companion computers. This is ongoing research.
