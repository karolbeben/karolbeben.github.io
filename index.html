About

I entered the UAV field through aircraft maintenance and avionics. Learning how an aircraft is built, configured and kept airworthy gave me a systems perspective that still guides my work. I can take a UAV from electronics, autopilot, payload and communications integration through mission preparation, permissions, ground checks, flight testing and data collection.

Working on remote sensing added another perspective: sensors, image acquisition and photogrammetry showed me that a successful flight is only part of a useful sensing mission. Today I am extending that practical foundation toward simulation, AI-based mission planning and coordination of UAV groups. I am especially interested in methods that can be evaluated on real aircraft, in field conditions.

I have worked with fixed-wing and VTOL aircraft, DJI platforms and custom Pixhawk-based UAVs. I am a UAV instructor and a radio amateur (SP7UAV).

Current research

My doctoral research at Warsaw University of Technology focuses on control and mission optimization for groups of UAVs in low-altitude remote sensing. I am studying coordinated mission planning and reinforcement learning, with an interest in companion computers and the transition from simulation to field tests. This research is ongoing.

Research question

How can several UAVs coordinate sensing missions under the constraints of real flight operations?

Approach

Mission planning, multi-agent methods, simulation and reinforcement learning.

Perspective

Aircraft integration, sensor data, operational safety and experimental validation.
