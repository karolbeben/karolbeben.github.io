What I do

I work across the UAV development and mission cycle:

  • Aircraft and avionics: integrate electronics, autopilots, electro-optical payloads and communications on fixed-wing and VTOL UAVs, including Pixhawk-based systems.
  • Remote sensing: select sensing approaches, plan data acquisition and work with imagery, photogrammetry and field data.
  • Flight operations: prepare missions and permissions, support continuing airworthiness, run ground and flight tests, and pilot UAVs, including BVLOS operations.
  • Research: develop simulation-based approaches to multi-UAV mission planning and investigate reinforcement learning for coordinated sensing.

I have more than ten years of experience with UAVs, over 1,000 hours in UAV operations, and UAV instructor qualifications. My earlier aircraft mechanic work informs my systems approach.

Current research

My doctoral research concerns AI-based control and mission optimization for groups of UAVs in low-altitude remote sensing. The focus is coordinated mission planning, reinforcement learning and the role of onboard companion computers. I use simulation to develop and examine these ideas, with field testing as a practical evaluation goal. This work is in progress.
