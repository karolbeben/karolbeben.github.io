About

For more than ten years I have worked with unmanned aircraft, from platform configuration to field operations. I integrate avionics, electronics, electro-optical payloads and communications; develop mission concepts; arrange operational permissions; support continuing airworthiness; and conduct ground and flight tests. My experience includes fixed-wing and VTOL platforms, DJI aircraft and custom UAVs based on Pixhawk flight controllers.

Earlier in my career I worked as an aircraft mechanic. That experience shaped a practical approach to research: a method has to fit the aircraft, its operators and the conditions of the mission. I am a UAV instructor and a radio amateur (SP7UAV).

Research

My doctoral work concerns control and mission optimization for UAV groups in low-altitude remote sensing. I am interested in coordinated coverage, reinforcement learning and the use of companion computers to support cooperation between aircraft. The research is in progress; simulation and flight experiments are part of the intended evaluation path.

Question

How can multiple UAVs coordinate sensing missions while meeting operational constraints?

Methods

Mission planning, multi-agent approaches, reinforcement learning and simulation.

Systems

Fixed-wing and VTOL aircraft, autopilots, onboard computing and flight-test data.
