01 / ABOUT

Research grounded
in engineering practice.

I am a research and development engineer at the Łukasiewicz Research Network – Institute of Aviation, working with unmanned aerial systems and remote sensing. My work connects platform integration, avionics, electro-optical payloads, flight operations and experimental data collection.

I am also pursuing a PhD at Warsaw University of Technology, focused on coordination and mission planning for multiple UAVs in low-altitude remote sensing. I am particularly interested in turning methods developed in simulation into systems that can be evaluated in the field.

Current focusMulti-UAV coordination · Mission planning · Fixed-wing UAVs · Experimental validation
